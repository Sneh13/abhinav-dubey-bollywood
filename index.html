Meet the Artist

Music that connects. Moments that stay.

Abhinav Dubey is a live singer and performer bringing the magic of Bollywood and Indian music to celebrations, weddings and corporate stages. From intimate acoustic sets to energetic stage performances, every show is shaped around the audience and the occasion.

With over two decades of musical experience, Abhinav blends a versatile song selection with a warm stage presence to create an experience guests remember long after the final note.
